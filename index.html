Is my money safe?

Yes. Here's exactly how.

🔒

Held, not sent

The brand's payment sits in a locked escrow account. It isn't ours and it isn't theirs — it can only go to you or back to them, by the rules everyone agreed to first.

Brands can't ghost you

If a brand goes quiet for 72 hours after your post is live, the money is released to you automatically. Silence doesn't cost you anything.

Every offer and agreement is timestamped and fingerprinted. If there's ever a dispute, the record decides it — not whoever shouts loudest.

Your contacts stay hidden

Phone numbers and emails in chat are automatically hidden. Deals that leave PAKT lose all this protection — so there's every reason to stay.

Common questions

No jargon.
Straight answers.

Is it really free to join?+
Yes. Joining, making your profile, being found by brands, and every creator tool costs nothing. We only take a 12% fee out of a deal when you actually get paid.
When exactly do I get my money?+
The moment your post is live and PAKT confirms it, the money is released to you — usually in under a second. If the brand goes quiet, it's released to you automatically after 72 hours.
What if the brand disappears after I post?+
They can't take the money back once it's in the vault. If they stop responding, PAKT releases it to you after 72 hours. You're covered either way.
Do I need to be big to join?+
No. PAKT is built for everyday creators, including smaller and regional accounts. Brands here are looking for real engagement, not just big follower counts.
What if we disagree on the work?+
Every offer and agreement is on the record with a timestamp and fingerprint. If there's a dispute, clear published rules decide it — was the post live, disclosed, and matching the brief. No favouritism.
Can I just deal with the brand directly instead?+
You can, but you'd lose the vault, instant pay, dispute protection, and your verified history. On PAKT the money is guaranteed. Off it, you're back to hoping. Staying is simply safer.
